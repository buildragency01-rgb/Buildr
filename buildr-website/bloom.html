About us

A friendly studio with serious skills

Bloom was started by two sisters who wanted makeup to feel light, personal and fun. Today our team of five artists works on brides, bridesmaids, shoots and everyday glow-ups.

Every client gets a patch test, a proper consultation and a look that suits their skin and their outfit.

Questions

Good to know

How early should I book a wedding date?

At least four to six weeks ahead during wedding season. Trials can be booked any time.

Do you travel to the venue?

Yes, within the city. Outstation travel is quoted separately.

Do you use products for sensitive skin?

Yes. Tell us about allergies when you book and we will patch test first.

How do I pay?

An advance confirms your date. The balance is paid on the day by UPI, card or cash.
