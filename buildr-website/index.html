Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you get in touch

How long does it take to build a website?

A simple landing page takes 3-4 days. Multi page and product websites usually take 5-7 days, depending on your content and feedback.

How much does a website cost?

Landing pages start at ₹2,999, business websites at ₹7,999 and product / store websites at ₹14,999. The final quote depends on your requirements.

Can I get a ready template customised for my brand?

Yes. Pick any design from our Template Gallery and we change colours, text, images and logo to match your brand.

Do you provide domain and hosting?

Yes. We set up your domain, hosting and business email. Yearly renewal is charged separately.

Will my website work well on mobile?

Absolutely. Every website we build is mobile first and tested on phones, tablets and desktops.

Can customers book or order through the website?

Yes. We can connect bookings and orders to your WhatsApp, and add UPI / Razorpay payments if you need them.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. Every plan includes support after launch, and you can add monthly maintenance for updates and backups.

How do I get started?

Fill the form on our Contact page or message us on WhatsApp. We will discuss your needs and share a demo design for approval.
